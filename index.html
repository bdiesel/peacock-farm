Peacock Farm Houses · Lexington, MA

Mid-Century Modern split-level homes designed by Compton & Pierce (MIT graduates). AIA First Award 1957. Low-pitched gable roof, vertical cedar siding, bands of windows — built across six Lexington neighborhoods.

Historic District: National Register of Historic Places (2012)
Design period: 1952–1979
Total properties: ~235 across 6 areas
Permit research: ViewpointCloud
